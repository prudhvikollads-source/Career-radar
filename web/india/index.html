How the savings goal works

Target: save ₹5 lakhs/month after taxes and living costs. Each job with a disclosed salary (Naukri shows "Lacs PA") is compared against ₹95,00,000/year gross, the pay typically needed in India to hit the target.

Notes & limitations

  • Postings older than 60 days are dropped; stale listings are usually already filled.
  • No visa sponsorship is needed for India roles — you are already authorized to work there.
