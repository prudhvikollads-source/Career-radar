How the savings goal works

Target: save ₹5 lakhs/month after taxes and living costs. Each job with a disclosed salary is compared against the gross annual pay typically needed in that country to hit the target (midpoint of the posted range). Gulf states levy no personal income tax, which is why the thresholds here are lower than in taxed markets.

Notes & limitations

  • Listings come from NaukriGulf (naukrigulf.com) public search pages — the Gulf's largest job board. No login; polite scraping only.
  • Postings older than 60 days are dropped; stale listings are usually already filled.
  • Salaries are rarely disclosed on NaukriGulf cards — most jobs show "Salary not disclosed". Figures shown are annualized from monthly USD ranges and converted to local currency.
  • Sponsorship flags are heuristic text matches — always confirm in the posting. Most Gulf employers sponsor work visas by default.
  • Only roles in the 6 Gulf countries are shown (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman).
