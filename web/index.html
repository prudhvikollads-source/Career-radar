How the savings goal works

Target: save ₹5 lakhs/month after taxes and living costs. Each job with a disclosed salary is compared against the gross annual pay typically needed in that country to hit the target (midpoint of the posted range).

Notes & limitations

  • Listings come from public company job boards (Greenhouse, Lever). No login, no scraping of private sites.
  • Salaries are mined from posting text and pay-transparency blocks; figures are annualized and converted to local currency at the day's FX rate.
  • Sponsorship flags are heuristic text matches ("visa sponsorship", "will sponsor", "no sponsorship") — always confirm in the posting.
  • Only roles in the 10 listed countries are shown; remote roles without a stated country are excluded.
  • Netherlands threshold assumes 30% ruling eligibility (€128k); without it, roughly €160k is needed.
